Privacy Policy

Shoyo Violin Lesson (“we”, “us” or “the studio”) handles personal information received through lesson enquiries and bookings in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

Your name, email address, information connected with your LINE account, country or time zone, preferred lesson dates and location, violin experience, whether you own an instrument, and any other information you provide in an enquiry.

How we use your information

We use this information to reply to enquiries, arrange bookings, provide lessons, share payment and studio details, send necessary communications, and improve our services.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law or where necessary to provide a service you have requested.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse.

Access, correction and deletion

If you ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, we will respond within a reasonable scope after confirming your identity.

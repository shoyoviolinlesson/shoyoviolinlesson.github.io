Instructor Profile

Shoyo Sakai

Born in Aichi, Japan, Shoyo began playing the violin at the age of six. After studying in the Music Course at Nagoya City Kikusato High School, he is currently majoring in violin at Toho Gakuen School of Music.

He teaches complete beginners and experienced players, with an emphasis on clear explanations, enjoyment of sound and steady progress at each student's pace.

Teachers

Fujio Kondo, Futaba Kato and Hamao Fujiwara. He currently studies with Tomoko Kato.

Awards and performance experience

Selected awards
2016 & 2017 Kariya International Music Competition — Excellence Award
2018 Kariya International Music Competition — Encouragement Award
2020 Beten Music Competition, Junior High School Division — 5th Prize
2023 Japan Classical Music Competition, Tokai Regional Final — 2nd Prize and highest placement

Performance experience
Served as concertmaster of the Toyota Junior Orchestra and his high school orchestra. Performances include Dvořák's Carnival Overture, with appearances at Tokugawa Garden, Nagoya City Archives and Okazaki Castle Noh Theatre.

Changes and cancellations
Schedule changes are free up to three days before the lesson. Lesson fees are non-refundable from two days before the lesson through the lesson day. Any studio cancellation fee is paid by the student. If the instructor must cancel, or the lesson cannot take place because of a disaster or major transport disruption, a rescheduled lesson or refund will be offered.
